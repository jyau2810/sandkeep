# Sandkeep quality bar

THESIS: a familiar photo becomes a small collectible to revisit.

OWN-WORLD: a quiet personal display plinth in warm daylight, ivory paper, brown ink, terracotta controls, clear glass, cork and fine colored grains. Serif Chinese display text; Chinese system sans for controls. The photograph supplies the vivid color.

STORY: inspect a finished example, choose a photo, play with loose color, assemble, turn to discover the full picture, return to the saved bottle.

FIRST VIEWPORT: show the bottle at an immediately recognizable scale. Controls recede beside it on desktop and below it on a narrow screen. A visible route opens the photo picker. There are no marketing metrics or claims.

FORM: code-led implementation, direction seed 889eefe6, grounded candidate 5 (personal display plinth). This is an implementation choice within the user's physical bottle brief. No comp was approved or used as a pixel specification.

The material floor is an opaque, legible granular photo with the original demo’s crisp pixel grains and dark gaps within an actual transparent 3D shell, cork and contact shadow. Dragging must change the visible circumference, not tilt a flat image. Glass must preserve readable colors. Loose motion and assembly should stay bounded and settle. Errors explain recovery. Mobile vertical scrolling remains usable.
